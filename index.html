The Challenge
INDIA'S
POST-HARVEST CRISIS
🌧️

Weather Dependency

Open sun drying is completely at the mercy of rain and clouds. A single unexpected shower can destroy an entire batch of produce in minutes.

🐛

Pest & Contamination

Exposed produce is vulnerable to dust, insects, birds, and animals — leading to contamination, health risks, and rejection by buyers.

⚖️

Uneven Drying

Traditional methods result in inconsistent moisture levels, reducing product quality, shortening shelf life, and fetching lower market prices.

💸

Income Losses

Unable to preserve quality, farmers are forced to sell produce quickly at distress prices — compounding financial hardship season after season.

30–40%

of India's fruits and vegetables are lost post-harvest every year — representing billions in economic losses for smallholder farmers and contributing to national food insecurity. Source: FAO, ICAR, NABARD reports

Our Solution
THE SUNDRY
SOLAR DRYER

A low-cost, enclosed solar dryer engineered specifically for Indian farmers — using the greenhouse effect, natural convection, and food-grade materials to deliver safe, hygienic, and efficient drying.

🏠

Enclosed Greenhouse Design

Fully sealed chamber traps solar heat via the greenhouse effect, elevating internal temperature and accelerating moisture evaporation uniformly.

☀

Zero-Cost Solar Power

Runs entirely on sunlight — no electricity, no fuel. After the one-time purchase, your operating cost is literally zero.

🛡️

Hygienic Food-Grade Trays

Multi-level food-grade drying trays keep produce elevated, protected, and uniformly exposed to warm air circulating through ventilation vents.

📦

Portable & Easy to Install

Lightweight, modular design that farmers can set up without tools. Move it between fields as needed. No permanent installation required.

HOW IT WORKS
01
🌾

Load Produce

Place freshly harvested crops on food-grade trays inside the enclosed solar dryer chamber.

02

Solar Heating

Sunlight enters through the transparent cover, trapped by the greenhouse effect, raising internal temperature significantly.

03

Convection vents circulate hot, dry air across all trays — removing moisture uniformly and efficiently.

Harvest perfectly dried, pest-free produce ready for storage, sale, or processing — at premium market prices.
